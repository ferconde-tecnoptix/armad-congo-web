Stage 1: INVIMA Sanitary Registration, Certification & Regulatory Roadmap

Commercializing Glanatec 0.4% Ophthalmic Solution (Ripasudil) in Colombia requires obtaining a formal Sanitary Registration (Registro Sanitario) from INVIMA (Instituto Nacional de Vigilancia de Medicamentos y Alimentos) under the classification of an Imported Pharmaceutical Product / New Chemical Entity (NCE), pursuant to Decrees 677/1995 and 843/2016. Because Ripasudil introduces a novel mechanism of action (Rho-kinase / ROCK inhibition) previously unregistered in the Colombian National Formulary, the submission must undergo full technical evaluation by INVIMA's Specialized Medical Commission (Comisión Revisora de Medicamentos).

Manufacturing Plant Certification (BPM / Good Manufacturing Practices): Prior to issuing the Sanitary Registration, INVIMA strictly mandates that Kowa’s primary manufacturing site in Japan (Nagoya/Fuji facilities) hold a recognized Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) certificate. Since Colombia maintains bilateral recognition criteria for stringent regulatory authorities (PMDA Japan), Taichi will submit Kowa’s official PMDA GMP certification under a document recognition workflow (Homologación de BPM).

Special Technical Requirements for Certification & Dossier:

  • Zone IVb Stability Validation: Colombia belongs to WHO Climatic Zone IVb (Hot and Humid: 30°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH). Kowa must provide accelerated (6-month) and long-term (12 to 36-month) stability testing conducted specifically under Zone IVb parameters to ensure active compound integrity.
  • CTD Format Dossier (Modules 1 to 5): Submission of complete Common Technical Document dossiers including Phase I-III clinical trial safety and efficacy data demonstrating trabecular meshwork outflow stimulation.
  • Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CPP): Legalized Free Sale Certificate issued by Japan PMDA, validated via Hague Apostille, accompanied by certified Spanish medical translations.
  • Sterility & Packaging Quality Protocol: Verification of LDPE multi-dose dropper container compatibility, preservative efficacy (Benzalkonium Chloride content stability), and extractable/leachable testing.

Key Regulatory Requirements Summary

  • GMP Certification (BPM): Kowa's manufacturing plant in Japan validated via PMDA document recognition.
  • Clinical Dossier: CTD Modules 1-5 detailing Phase III trials in open-angle glaucoma and ocular hypertension.
  • Quality & Stability Data: Zone IVb (30°C / 75% RH) climate chamber stability proof.
  • CPP (Free Sale Certificate): PMDA certificate with Hague Apostille and official Spanish translation.
  • Legalization & Scientific Dossier: Local regulatory filing managed by Taichi’s specialized team.

Stage 2: Cost Structure, Price Waterfall Architecture & Expiry Risk Control

To establish commercial viability, a rigorous Price Waterfall Architecture has been modeled from Ex-Factory FOB Nagoya/Fuji through local import clearance, wholesale distribution, and retail patient pricing in Colombia. Each tier in the pricing ladder reflects local duty structures, freight logistics, distributor operating margins, and Colombian tax regulations.

Detailed Explanation of Pricing Levels (Referencing Table 2.1):

  • Level 1 - FOB Kowa (Japan): Agreed ex-factory transfer price of $13.50 USD (54,000 COP @ 4,000 COP/USD) per 5ml bottle.
  • Level 2 - International Cold-Chain Air Freight & Cargo Insurance (CIF): $0.95 USD per unit (7.0% of FOB). Temperature-controlled air logistics (2°C to 8°C) are strictly required to protect the solution during transit from Japan to Bogota Airport.
  • Level 3 - Import Tariff & Port/Customs Clearance: $0.45 USD per unit (3.1% of CIF). Under the Colombia-Japan bilateral framework, HS Code 3004.90 benefits from a 0% import tariff, leaving only national customs brokerage (SIA), DIAN registration fees, and airport cold-storage handling.
  • Level 4 - Landed Cost (COGS Bogota Warehouse): Summing Levels 1 through 3 yields a total delivered cost of $14.90 USD (~59,600 COP) per unit delivered in Taichi's central GDP warehouse.
  • Level 5 - Taichi Wholesale Price (Ex-Distributor): Set at $26.00 USD (~104,000 COP), generating a solid 42.7% Gross Margin ($11.10 USD / unit) for Taichi to fund local commercial operations, field force salaries, and medical seeding.
  • Level 6 - Target Public Selling Price (PVP / Retail): Set at $42.00 USD (~168,000 COP). Under Article 477 of the Colombian Tax Code (Estatuto Tributario), human ophthalmic prescription medications are 100% exempt from Value-Added Tax (IVA 0%). This allows retail pharmacy chains (e.g., Cruz Verde, Farmatodo) and IPS dispensaries an attractive 38.1% Retail Margin ($16.00 USD) without inflating final patient cost.

Inventory Shelf-Life & Near-Expiry Strategy

Glanatec has a total unopened shelf-life of 36 months. However, to prevent financial write-offs due to stock expiration in Colombian warehouses:

  • Dynamic Batched Air Shipments: Import in smaller, quarterly batches (1,500–3,000 units) rather than annual bulk sea freight, ensuring warehouse stock always retains >28 months shelf-life upon arrival.
  • FEFO GDP Warehouse Protocol: Enforce strict First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) automated picking in Taichi's central GDP hub.
  • KOL Sample Conversion Protocol: Any inventory batch reaching 9 months remaining shelf-life is automatically transferred from commercial stock to the Medical Affairs department as official physician samples for seeding.
Target Expiry Scrap Rate: < 1.5% of total imported volume

Stage 3: 5-Year Sales Projections & Market Channel Entry Strategy

The commercial rollout for Glanatec follows a phased 5-year ramp (as illustrated in Figure 3.1), moving from initial medical seeding in Year 1 to broad institutional access by Year 5. Total volume expands from 4,000 units ($104,000 USD gross revenue) in Year 1 to 48,000 units ($1,248,000 USD gross revenue) in Year 5.

Detailed Market Channel Segmentation & Strategic Entry Plan (Referencing Figure 3.2):

  • Prepaid Health & Private Policies (45% Market Share - ~21,600 units/yr in Y5): Target Entities: Colsanitas, SURA Prepagada, Medplus, and Coomeva Prepagada.
    Entry Strategy: Present clinical dossiers to direct medical evaluation boards. Because prepaid plans allow rapid physician autonomy without EPS bureaucratic hurdles, Glanatec can be prescribed immediately as an add-on therapy for refractory patients seeking to avoid invasive surgery.
  • Private Out-of-Pocket Consultations (35% Market Share - ~16,800 units/yr in Y5): Target Entities: High-income cash-pay patients in top specialized eye clinics (e.g., Clinica Barraquer in Bogota, Clofan in Medellin, Clinica de Oftalmologia de Cali).
    Entry Strategy: Focus on top-tier private practice glaucomatologists and cornea specialists. Fulfilled through premium retail pharmacy partners (Cruz Verde, Farmatodo, Pasteur).
  • EPS / Institutional Non-PBS System (20% Market Share - ~9,600 units/yr in Y5): Target Entities: Health Provision Entities (EPS SURA, EPS Sanitas, Compensar) servicing advanced patients via the MIPRES platform.
    Entry Strategy: Position Glanatec for multi-refractory glaucoma cases failing 3+ standard IOP drugs or presenting corneal endothelial dystrophy (Fuchs Dystrophy), justifying special institutional reimbursement.

Stage 4: Field Force Organization, Medical Seeding & Commercial OPEX Justification

To achieve initial market adoption for a novel molecule like Ripasudil, Taichi will deploy a targeted Key Opinion Leader (KOL) Seeding Program paired with a specialized sales representative. Rather than mass marketing, investment is concentrated on the top 120 prescribers across Colombia's four primary metropolitan centers (Bogota, Medellin, Cali, Barranquilla).

Detailed Justification of Projected Commercial Costs (Referencing Table 4.1):

  • Physician Sample Stock ($11,520 USD / Year): 1,440 sample units per year (12 units/year per physician, or 1 unit/month/doctor) purchased from Kowa at a special sample rate of $8.00 USD/unit. Justification: In refractory glaucoma, specialists require hands-on patient trials to observe IOP-lowering efficacy and confirm corneal safety before issuing recurring prescriptions.
  • Sample Cold-Chain Freight & Local Delivery ($3,000 USD / Year): 12 monthly refrigerated delivery runs (2°C–8°C) to top eye clinics in target cities ($250 USD/month). Justification: Maintains product integrity from Taichi's central warehouse directly to physician offices.
  • Senior Ophthalmic Product Specialist ($38,400 USD / Year): Full compensation for 1 dedicated representative ($3,200 USD/month total package including base salary, statutory social benefits, mobility allowance, and performance bonuses). Justification: Requires a high-caliber representative capable of technical peer-to-peer discussions with glaucoma chairs. This specialist will also train regional sub-distributor reps as volume scales.
  • Academic Symposia & Scientific Launch Dinners ($12,000 USD / Year): 2 national academic events annually (e.g., Facocaribe and the Colombian Glaucoma Congress). Justification: Position Glanatec scientifically via international guest speakers and peer-reviewed data presentation.
